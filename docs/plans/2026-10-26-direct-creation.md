## Acceptance

An author can choose a step, edit its caption and framing, see representative
feedback, adjust its hold, undo an edit and update the recording without
opening documentation or scrolling through other steps' forms. Aim for warm
still feedback within a second on the notes fixture; record measured results
and remaining limits rather than claiming a universal latency guarantee.

Recipes explain their intended structure and approximate duration before
applying. Demonstrated changes show what would replace or extend the story
before saving, retain the previous recording during verification, and never
accept or publish media automatically.

Run appropriate source-preservation and browser regressions, inspect desktop
and mobile pages, rebuild the visual gallery if rendering changes, and verify
documentation, syntax and wheel contents. Outside-user testing remains
deferred under the existing plan.
